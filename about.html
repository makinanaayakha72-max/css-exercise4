I started Vanguard Comics from a bedroom shelf overflowing with back issues. What began as swapping comics with friends turned into a small shop for capes, collectibles and first editions.

Every product is picked because I would proudly display it myself. I care about honest materials, fair prices and giving fellow fans a place to geek out.

Fun fact: The shop is named after the first hero I ever drew, at age nine.
